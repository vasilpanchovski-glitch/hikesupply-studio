Before you go

Plan each park visit in 5 minutes

Rules, fees and road openings change from season to season. A quick check on the park's own page saves a wasted drive.

Check the park's alerts

Every park has a page on nps.gov with current alerts: road and trail closures, fire, flooding, snow and construction. Read it the week you go and again the morning you leave.

Reservations and timed entry

Some parks need a reservation for entry, a scenic road, a cave tour, a ferry or a campground at busy times. The park's page says if you need one. Most are booked on recreation.gov.

Passes and fees

Many parks charge an entrance fee and some are free. The America the Beautiful annual pass covers entrance at every national park. Prices, discounts and extra fees for visitors from outside the US changed in 2026: check nps.gov for current prices and who qualifies.

Free entrance days

The National Park Service sets a few fee-free days each year. The dates and who they apply to can change, so check nps.gov before you plan around one.

Cell signal is weak or missing in many parks. Download maps before you go, carry water, and tell someone your plan and when you will be back.
