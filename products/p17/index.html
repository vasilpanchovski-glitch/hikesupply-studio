Pocket Trail Log

One sheet of paper, three folds and one cut make an 8-page booklet that fits a shirt pocket. Six editions to print as often as you like, with a short prompt for every hike. Miles or kilometers, feet or meters.

12 pages6 booklets to printLetter and A4
Start here

One sheet, one cut, one booklet

Each printable sheet folds into its own 8-page booklet, 2.75 x 4.25 in (7 x 11 cm). Print the edition you want, as many times as you like.

1

Print and turn

Print one sheet at actual size. Lay it printed side up with the long side across, so the upside-down pages are on top.

2

Crease eight boxes

Fold in half top to bottom, crease, open. Fold in half side to side, then each end to the middle. Open it flat.

3

One cut

Fold in half side to side again. From the folded edge, cut along the solid line to the center crease. Open.

4

Long fold

Fold in half top to bottom along the long crease, with the printing on the outside.

5

Push into a plus

Hold both ends and push them toward each other. The cut opens into a diamond, then a plus shape.

6

Fold it shut

Fold the pages around so the cover is on top. Crease the spine. Done, no glue.

Print tips

Print one page at a time (for example "page 3"), single-sided, at actual size or 100%. Plain copy paper folds best. Kraft or colored paper looks great for gifts, card stock is too thick. On A4 paper, use the A4 file and fold on the printed dotted lines. Write in pencil or a fine pen.
