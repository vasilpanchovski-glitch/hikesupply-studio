How to use this journal

This journal is made to be printed and filled in by hand. Fill in a log page after each hike, while the details are still fresh. A few lines are enough.

What is inside

  • About me and my goals for the year
  • A 52-week tracker and a monthly distance tracker
  • A trail bucket list and a hall of fame for your best hikes
  • 52 hike log pages, one hike per page
  • Gear notes, favorite trail snacks, notes and a year-end look back

Printing tips

Paper. Heavier paper takes ink better. Try 28 to 32 lb (105 to 120 gsm) paper, or light card stock around 65 lb (176 gsm) if your printer accepts it.

Size. Print at "Actual size" on US Letter. An A4 version is included; if you print the Letter file on A4, choose "Fit to page".

Binding. Three-hole punch the pages into a binder, or ask a print shop to spiral-bind them. The margins leave room for both.

Only what you need. Print a handful of log pages at a time and reprint more whenever you like. A printer-friendly version with less ink is included too.

Units

Every log page has room for miles and kilometers, feet and meters. Use the one you know, or both. The quick conversions are on the distance tracker page.

Filling in a log page

  1. Jot the basics at the trailhead or in the car: date, trail, start time.
  2. Copy distance and elevation gain from the trail sign, park map or your app. Rough numbers are fine.
  3. Write the best moment the same evening, while you still remember the small things.
  4. Use the sketch box for a quick map, a leaf rubbing, a ticket stub or a printed photo.
Before every hike, check the weather and trail conditions with the park or land manager, and tell someone where you are going and when you plan to be back.
