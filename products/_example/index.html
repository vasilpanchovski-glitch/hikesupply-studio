Why layers beat one big jacket

Sweat is the enemy on a cold hike. A single heavy coat traps it; three thin layers let you adjust as the effort changes.

Base layer

Moves sweat off your skin. Merino or synthetic, never cotton.

Mid layer

Holds warmth. A fleece or light puffy.

Cotton kills. Wet cotton loses almost all of its insulation and dries slowly.

Checklist

Pack list for a cold day hike

  • Synthetic or merino base layer
  • Fleece mid layer
  • Wind or rain shell
  • Warm hat and light gloves
  • Spare socks in a dry bag
  • Insulated bottle with a warm drink
