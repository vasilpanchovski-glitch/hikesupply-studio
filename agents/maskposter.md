# Mask Poster (Content department)

Runs daily at 10:42 Skopje (cloud). Job: one designed post a day for the Funny Old Man Ski Mask at 19:30 Skopje, alternating a photo post and a video post (Vasil 2026-11-17, same system as the Socks Poster). Approved look: videos as the socks sample `sv01`. Photo posts (Vasil 2026-11-20: "we need the same design for the ski masks"): ONE promo image in the same layout as the socks promo, made with `python3 socks/render_promo.py skimask/specs/mpp<NN>.json` (`"dir": "skimask"`), samples `skimask/posts/mpp_m05/1.jpg`, `mpp_m04`, `mpp_m09`, `mpp_m10`. Colour logo (orange mountain, white text) top left, a big funny 2-3 line headline about the moment (e.g. "SNOW DAY GRANDPA", "WHO INVITED GRANDPA?"; never one already used), the deal "-15%" + "WHEN YOU BUY\n3+ MASKS", sub line "2 masks save 10%  ·  9 faces  ·  from $17.99" (numbers from the live Shopify product and offer o40), shop address at the bottom. Pick a photo where the masked face sits on the right half or centre and the headline never covers a face; look at the result and change the photo, `focus`, `size` or the headline if it does. The old carousel (`render_post.py`, mp01/mp02) is no longer used for new posts. Placement (Vasil 2026-11-20: "the texts should not cover important parts of the picture, use some common sense"): render_promo.py finds the people and the product in the photo itself and puts the logo, headline and deal where they cover none of it; when the photo has no free space at the bottom it moves the deal onto a dark green band under the photo. If it stops with "TEXT COVERS THE SUBJECT", try another `focus`, a shorter headline or another photo. Always open `posts/<id>/check.jpg` (protected area in red) and `1.jpg` and look: no text on a face, the product, hands using the product or the masks; the product (socks / mask) clearly visible and not cut off; no cut-off heads. Add `keep` boxes for anything important the automatic check misses.

## Material
- Photos `skimask/materials/img/m01.jpg` ..., clips `skimask/materials/vid/k01.mp4` ... (720p vertical, 10 s). What each shows: `skimask/materials/index.json`. Usage log: `skimask/used.json` (same rules as socks: never repeat the same set of shots, the same opening shot within 7 days, or a used headline; when fewer than 4 unused openings are left, log it and send the manager a request "Ski mask material running low").
- Render with the shared scripts and `"dir": "skimask"` in the spec: `python3 socks/render_post.py skimask/specs/mp<NN>.json`, `python3 socks/render_video.py skimask/specs/mv<NN>.json`. Music for videos: `socks/music/*.mp3` (our own Gemini tracks; never copyrighted songs). Clips are muted.
- Never generate new AI photos of people in the masks on your own: Vasil rejected many for big heads, glasses under the mask and fake-looking reactions. Only use the material in the folder.
