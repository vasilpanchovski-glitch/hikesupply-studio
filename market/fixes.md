# What buyers hate about competitors, and how we beat it

One line per theme seen 3+ times: pain | our honest answer | owner agent.

Updated 2026-10-31 (137 complaints so far; Oct 31 added traction cleats (Yaktrax, MICROspikes), Decathlon pole and pack, a journal; earlier: this run added the budget pole and sock benchmarks: Cascade Mountain Tech and Darn Tough, from OutdoorGearLab, Trustpilot, REI, review blogs and a forum; Amazon and Reddit still blocked from the cloud).

- Pole locks slip, pole collapses under weight (15) | Hunter vets pole suppliers for lock type and lock complaints in their own reviews; Editor adds a short "how to lock it and check it" section to our Folding Trekking Pole page; content shows the 5-second lock check before a descent | hunter, editor, strategist
- No clear assembly or height instructions (7) | Our pole page explains how to unfold, set the length and lock, and to stay inside the marked range; same text can ride in the Beginner's Hiking Guide | editor, seo
- Missing parts on arrival (8, incl. a sock multipack that arrived as one pair, a filter bottle without its lid gasket, a dry bag without its advertised phone case) | Every gear listing has an "In the box" row (our pole: 1 pole, single); Hunter checks supplier reviews for missing sections before import | editor, hunter
- Breaks quickly, feels cheap (12, incl. sock cuffs stretching, pole shafts nicked by rocks, dry bag seams fraying and roll-top clips breaking) | Honest specs only (material, weight, length range), no "heavy duty" claims we can't back | editor
- Hydration bladder leaks (3) | Bladder page gets a fill-and-squeeze leak check before the first hike and how to seat the cap and tube; Hunter checks leak complaints before restocking | editor, hunter
- Collapsible bottle drips or feels flimsy (3) | Bottle page says plainly it is soft silicone, how the cap closes, and keeps claims we can verify (drop "no plastic taste" unless the supplier shows a test) | editor
- Park list incomplete, wrong or out of date (6) | Our guides and any parks tracker carry a "checked against nps.gov on <date>" line, cover all 63 parks incl. American Samoa and Virgin Islands, and get a yearly update | maker, scout
- Baskets, tips or straps wear out or fall off (8, rival pole brands Montem, Hiker Hunger and Cascade Mountain Tech) | Our pole page says exactly which tip and basket come with it; Hunter checks the supplier offers spare tips or baskets and prefers screw-on baskets; Sales Planner can pair spare tips honestly if we stock them | editor, hunter, merch
- Park guide or journal is generic: a blank logbook when buyers expected park info and maps (5) | Every park product carries real park-specific pages (named trails, distance in mi and km, our own simple maps, when to go), and the product page shows previews of every page so buyers know exactly what they get | maker, scout
- Filter straw clogs, flow drops, hard to suck, worse in cold (5) | Our Personal Water Filter Straw page says plainly how to use it (drink at the source or from a bottle), how to backflush if the maker allows it, to pre-filter silty water through a cloth, to keep it from freezing and to replace it when flow drops; no claims beyond the supplier's real specs (e.g. viruses) | editor, hunter, strategist
- Emergency bivy: condensation, no insulation (3) | Our Emergency Bivy page says honestly it is a backup shelter, not a sleeping bag: expect some condensation, add layers and something under you, and lists the real size in cm and inches | editor
- Headlamp dies or fades fast in the cold (4) | Our Motion-Sensor Headlamp page says plainly that cold drains any battery faster: charge it full, keep it in an inside pocket between uses, carry a backup light; no run-time numbers we have not tested; videos and the Winter Guide teach the same | editor, strategist, seo
- Headlamp buttons or panels hard to use with gloves (3) | Our headlamp's wave-on motion sensor is a real answer: say it on the page and show it in a cold-morning video (gloves on, wave, light on); Hunter prefers big buttons or a sensor for any future light | strategist, editor, hunter
- Gloves, gaiters and socks too thin, not warm enough (4) | Any winter accessory we list states the real fabric and thickness and says honestly what temperatures it suits (liner vs warm glove); Hunter checks warmth complaints in supplier reviews before import | hunter, editor
- Product does not match the listing: 'heated' gloves not heated, run times for other batteries, dry bags of the wrong size or not waterproof at all (5) | We never list 'heated' or run-time claims we cannot verify; Hunter skips listings whose title promises a feature the photos and specs do not show | hunter, editor
- Warranty or fault claims are a hassle: slow replies, cut-up proof photos, buyer pays return shipping (3, all on Darn Tough socks) | We do not promise a lifetime warranty we cannot back. Instead one plain, short promise on gear pages that Vasil approves first, e.g. a faulty or wrong item is replaced or refunded from one photo, no return shipping on our mistake; the Order Desk answers within a day | manager, orderdesk, editor
- Dry bag leaks (3: a damp-contents complaint on Earth Pak, a budget bag wet after 60 minutes in a test, and leaks when the roll-top is folded fewer than 3 times) | Our Waterproof Dry Bag page says how to close it (roll at least 3 times, then clip), states the real size in L and cm/in and what is in the box, and claims only the water rating the supplier really shows; Hunter checks for taped or welded seams and leak reviews before restocking | editor, hunter
- Traction cleats slip off the boot heel or the straps and chain joints tear within days (3, Yaktrax Quick Chains at REI, 2.5 stars) | We sell no traction yet, so no product claim. Content teaches the honest fix: fit them on your own boots at home, heel strap fully over the heel, test on a step; for steep or hard ice, real microspikes or crampons, not light chains. If the Hunter ever lists traction: steel spikes on a solid elastomer harness, no fabric-to-chain joints, reviews checked for 'slipped off' and 'tore' | strategist, hunter
- Pole grip slides down the shaft or is uncomfortable/slippery (3, incl. Decathlon MH500 2026 reviews) | Editor says on our pole page what the grip is really made of; Hunter checks supplier reviews for 'grip loose' before any pole restock | editor, hunter
