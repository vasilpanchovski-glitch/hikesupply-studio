# What buyers hate about competitors, and how we beat it

One line per theme seen 3+ times: pain | our honest answer | owner agent.

Updated 2026-10-17 (first complaint run, 42 complaints, Walmart, Etsy, Goodreads; Amazon and Reddit blocked from the cloud).

- Pole locks slip, pole collapses under weight (13) | Hunter vets pole suppliers for lock type and lock complaints in their own reviews; Editor adds a short "how to lock it and check it" section to our Folding Trekking Pole page; content shows the 5-second lock check before a descent | hunter, editor, strategist
- No clear assembly or height instructions (6) | Our pole page explains how to unfold, set the length and lock, and to stay inside the marked range; same text can ride in the Beginner's Hiking Guide | editor, seo
- Missing parts on arrival (5) | Every gear listing has an "In the box" row (our pole: 1 pole, single); Hunter checks supplier reviews for missing sections before import | editor, hunter
- Breaks quickly, feels cheap (3) | Honest specs only (material, weight, length range), no "heavy duty" claims we can't back | editor
- Hydration bladder leaks (3) | Bladder page gets a fill-and-squeeze leak check before the first hike and how to seat the cap and tube; Hunter checks leak complaints before restocking | editor, hunter
- Collapsible bottle drips or feels flimsy (3) | Bottle page says plainly it is soft silicone, how the cap closes, and keeps claims we can verify (drop "no plastic taste" unless the supplier shows a test) | editor
- Park list incomplete or out of date (3) | Our guides and any parks tracker carry a "checked against nps.gov on <date>" line, cover all 63 parks incl. American Samoa and Virgin Islands, and get a yearly update | maker, scout
